STEP 26 — GEORUSH 3-AGENT DEEP RESEARCH

Added /api/research/multi-agent and a 3-Agent Deep Research UI.
Agent 1: Competitor Researcher
Agent 2: SEO + Keyword Researcher
Agent 3: Critical Reviewer + Report Synthesizer

GEORUSH itself collects public search snippets and competitor/target webpages. The three AI roles analyze the same evidence pack. The final reviewer cross-checks the first two agents and creates the report. No ChatGPT login, browser cookie, password or session scraping is used.

Requires GEMINI_API_KEY on the backend.
